About Creative Faith · Since 2008

18 years changes how you see a brief.

After enough launches, packaging systems, reports, campaigns, digital journeys and customer programs, you stop seeing deliverables as isolated jobs. You see what has to work together.

2008Creative Faith began with one belief: understand the problem before deciding the output.

What we believe

We don't start with design. We start with the problem.

Creative Faith is a strategic creative agency in Jakarta. Since 2008, we have worked with organizations across FMCG, healthcare, retail, property, finance and emerging sectors — helping turn business and communication challenges into clear strategic and creative responses.

Direction first

Before deciding what to make, we define what needs to change, who needs to understand it and what should happen next.

Output second

Identity, packaging, reports, campaigns, websites and commerce are expressions of the answer — not the starting point.

What experience changes

Experience should improve judgment, not just increase the portfolio.

Eighteen years does not mean repeating the same way of working. It means getting better at seeing the real brief, choosing what matters and protecting the idea through execution.

How we work today

Senior thinking. Creative judgment. Technology when useful.

The tools have changed dramatically since 2008. Our responsibility has not. We still need to understand the business problem, choose the right direction and make sure the execution does what it is supposed to do.

AI and automation can help us move faster, explore more possibilities and build more responsive commerce experiences. They stay behind the work unless they create meaningful value for the client or customer.
