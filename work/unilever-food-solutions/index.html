From business program to participation.

01 — The challenge

Make a business program matter at restaurant level.

The challenge was to connect a B2B relationship with restaurant partners to experiences that customers could actually notice, understand and join.

Customer engagement programs only work when the business objective can travel through the partner network and still feel relevant at the point of experience. That requires communication, participation mechanics and execution to work together.

The real question: how do you connect business, restaurant and customer interests in one activation system?
02 — The thinking

Design the chain of participation.

Business value at one end. Customer experience at the other. The program has to connect both.

Creative Faith supported UFS with recurring engagement initiatives that translated business objectives into restaurant-facing programs and customer participation. The thinking focused on continuity, usability for restaurant partners and experiences that could encourage participation and loyalty.

Business objective → Restaurant partner → Customer experience → Participation → Loyalty
Selected original UFS activation and product-support materials. The work extended across recurring customer-engagement initiatives rather than a single isolated campaign.
What the response needed to do

Create programs that restaurants could activate and customers could join.

  • Customer engagement program direction
  • Restaurant participation mechanics
  • Campaign and promotional communication
  • Content and menu support
  • Point-of-experience materials
  • Multi-year activation continuity
03 — The response

Connect every layer of the experience.

The response linked communication from the business level through restaurant partners to customer-facing activation. Materials were designed to help restaurants participate, explain the offer and invite customers into the experience.

Across multiple years, that approach supported an ongoing customer-engagement ecosystem rather than treating every activation as a disconnected one-off.

An activation becomes stronger when every participant understands what role they play in it.
