Make relevance start with understanding.

Using empathy-led storytelling and community engagement to make communication more relevant, targeted and useful.

01 — The challenge

Improve relevance by understanding the audience better.

Limited customer understanding can make communication less relevant, harder to target and more expensive to sustain.

The brief was not simply to increase content output. The deeper need was to create stronger audience connection and a platform that could help the brand communicate with greater empathy and precision.

The real question: how do you build communication around what the audience actually cares about?
02 — The thinking

Empathy before media.

Better targeting starts with a better reason for people to care.

Creative Faith developed #JagahatiIbu as a customer-centered platform built around empathy-led storytelling, community engagement and relevant content. The platform gave communication a more specific audience lens and helped strengthen the connection between content, community and media efficiency.

Customer understanding → Empathy → Relevant content → Community engagement → Better targeting

03 — The response

Give the communication a human center.

The response was to create a platform with a clear audience idea rather than a loose stream of posts. #JagahatiIbu provided a central territory for storytelling and community engagement.

That clearer strategic center supported stronger relevance and audience connection while giving targeting and media activity a more useful foundation.

When the audience becomes clearer, the communication becomes sharper.
How the system moved

From understanding to better engagement.

The platform connected insight, storytelling, community and media learning into one communication loop.

Relevance showed up in the numbers.

The case demonstrates the value of customer-centered communication: a stronger strategic idea can improve both the quality of audience connection and the efficiency of how media is used.
