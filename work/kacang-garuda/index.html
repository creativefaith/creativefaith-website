Kacang Garuda · National Campaign Activation

Turn packaging into participation.

Connecting product purchase, campaign engagement and brand interaction through one football-themed customer experience.

PackagingCampaign ActivationDigital ParticipationCustomer Engagement
ClientKacang Garuda
ExpertiseBrand & Business / Digital & Commerce
PlatformFootball-themed activation
RolePackaging + digital participation
01 — The challenge

Move engagement beyond product consumption.

A major football moment created attention. The opportunity was to turn that attention into customer participation around the product.

The challenge was to strengthen customer engagement during a major global sporting event while creating reasons for people to interact with the brand beyond simply buying and consuming the product.

The real question: how can the pack become the beginning of the campaign, not the end of the transaction?
02 — The thinking

Use the product as the entry point.

Packaging already reaches the customer. Make it carry the invitation to participate.

Creative Faith developed a football-themed activation platform connecting packaging, digital participation and brand interaction. The idea treated the pack as an active campaign touchpoint that could lead customers into a broader experience.

Purchase → Packaging cue → Digital participation → Brand interaction
Selected original Kacang Garuda football campaign work across packaging and digital communication.
What the response needed to do

Connect the physical product to a broader campaign experience.

  • Campaign platform direction
  • Football-themed packaging
  • Customer participation mechanics
  • Digital experience
  • Multi-touchpoint campaign communication
  • Brand interaction beyond purchase
03 — The response

Make the pack part of the media system.

The response connected the physical product with digital and campaign touchpoints so customers could move from purchase into participation. Packaging carried campaign cues; digital content extended the interaction.

That gave the brand more opportunities to engage customers beyond the moment of consumption and made the product itself part of the activation infrastructure.

The product is already in the customer’s hands. Use that moment to open the next interaction.
How the system moved

From purchase to participation.

The customer journey was designed to move naturally from the physical pack into a broader brand experience.

The campaign made packaging work harder than a product container.

By connecting physical packaging with digital participation, the activation created a more integrated customer journey and more opportunities for brand interaction.
