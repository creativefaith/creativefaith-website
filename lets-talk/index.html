A better first conversation
What are you trying to change?

That question usually gets us further than starting with “what do you want us to design?”

Before the output

Give us the context.

A useful brief does not need to be polished. Four things are enough to begin: what is happening, who matters, what needs to change, and when the work needs to move.

01 · Context

What is happening?

Business situation, market context, existing problem, internal trigger or opportunity.

02 · Audience

Who needs to respond?

Customer, stakeholder, employee, investor, partner or another group that matters to the outcome.

03 · Change

What should be different?

What should people understand, believe, choose, do or experience differently after the work.

04 · Timing

When does it need to move?

A hard launch date, reporting cycle, campaign window or simply the point when a decision needs to be made.
